# Start here

Petra's own picks from [Site of Sites](https://www.siteofsites.co/), October 2026. **Look at these first**, before the galleries, before the other research files, before anything a model suggests. They're the closest to the portfolios she wants to make: personal, made by one person, and each with a voice of its own.

Open the live site, not the gallery entry. Scroll slowly, hover everything, and note what moves and why. Take one technique per site and change it to fit, not the look.

## How to use this list

- `site-concept` and `design-review` should open these before proposing anything.
- When a site here gets researched properly, pin it in `content/inspo/` (with screenshots, as the CLAUDE.md describes) and link the pin id in the table.
- Add to the list only what Petra picks herself.
